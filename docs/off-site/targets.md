# Off-site targets (verified 2026-11-22)

Each row was checked against the site's own pages in this session. All three are free. None is a
"backlink trick": the value is real people finding the tools. Submit from your own accounts.

| Target | URL | What it accepts | Cost | Rules that matter | Page to point at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AlternativeTo | https://alternativeto.net/faq | A software or web app, listed by an account holder: sign up, user icon > "Suggest new application", fill Platforms, License, Description, Tags | Free to list (third-party reviews mention an optional $5 priority review and a review backlog of months; not confirmed on its own FAQ) | Its terms ban spam, advertising disguised as recommendations, and vote/review manipulation. Third-party reports disagree on whether the outbound link is nofollow, so do not count on it as a backlink | https://toolkitties.com/ (one entry for the whole site; do not add every tool) |
| Hacker News "Show HN" | https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html | Something you made that people can try. Title must begin "Show HN". Landing pages and things not ready to try are not allowed | Free | Honest description only; no asking for upvotes (see https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) | One working tool, e.g. https://toolkitties.com/merge-pdf, not the homepage |
| Product Hunt | https://www.producthunt.com/launch | A launched product with a personal (not company) account; URL, thumbnail, description up to 260 characters, at least 2 gallery images | Free | You may not ask people to upvote (ask them to visit and comment); paying people to hunt or promote is against the rules; a product can only be launched once, so prepare first | https://toolkitties.com/ |

## Not included on purpose
- Reddit and other communities: every subreddit sets its own self-promotion rules (karma limits,
  allowed days). Read the rules of the specific community before posting, and only post where the
  tools genuinely answer a question people there ask. No target was added because none was checked.
- Paid listings, link exchanges, and any "submit to 100 directories" service.

## Notes
- Add `?utm_source=<target>&utm_medium=listing` to submitted links so GSC/analytics shows which
  listing sends visitors.
